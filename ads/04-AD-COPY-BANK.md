# AD COPY BANK, AI Ads Webinar

*Meta primary text (long + short) and mobile-short headlines to cycle across the recorded videos. Voice: SalesGenius, capability-first, honest proof. Written for the scroll, the Sabri Suby way: one idea per line, hard line breaks, short punchy sentences, bullet lists, plenty of white space. Long is fine if it's exciting. Dense paragraphs are not. No em-dashes. Congruent with the funnel promise on every block.*

**The one promise every ad lands on:**
> Get your own buyer and seller leads on demand, without an agency, without being techy, because AI now builds the ads for you. Free live workshop, I'll show you exactly how.

**How the media buyer uses this file**
- **Start with an ALL-PURPOSE master ad (Part 0).** Written to work under ANY of the 15 videos. Run one strong body across every creative and test the video, not the copy. Default to these.
- The angle-specific blocks (BODY-xx, Part 1) echo a specific video, or test copy against a proven winner later. They pair with any video too.
- Every ad = one video + one primary text + one headline + one description + one CTA button.
- LONG on cold (they pre-sell). SHORT on warm, mobile, and Reels/Stories.
- Put the copy ID in the ad name so results read by copy, not just by video.

**Formatting rules (so nobody re-bloats these)**
- One idea per line. Hit Enter often.
- Never more than 2 lines in a row without a break.
- Use bullets for any list.
- First line must land the hook before the "See more" cut (~125 characters).
- Keep the line breaks when you paste into Meta. That white space is the readability.

**Meta field cheat-sheet**
- **Primary text:** ~125 characters show before "See more" on mobile. Front-load the hook.
- **Headline:** bold line under the video. Only ~27 to 40 characters show on mobile before it truncates. Keep it tight (Part 4).
- **Description:** optional, some placements only (Part 5).

## Compliance + brand guardrails (read before you ship a variant)
- **Honest-proof rule:** never imply a client DIY'd the result. We ran the system; AI now lets them run it themselves.
- **No income guarantees on the ad.** The GCI numbers are real client results, framed as results, not promises.
- **Fair Housing:** no targeting or copy referencing protected classes. Broad + interest targeting only.
- **No em-dashes** (brand voice).
- **Readability:** one idea per line, break often, bullets for lists. Never re-bloat into paragraphs.
- **Congruency:** every new headline or body still lands on the one promise at the top. Change the hook, never the promise.
