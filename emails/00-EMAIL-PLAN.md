## The four quadrants, pre-filled from our sales process
*(React/add anything your closers hear that's missing. This is the raw material every email pulls from.)*

### Q1, Questions (surface FAQ)
- Do I need to be tech-savvy?
- How much do I need to spend on ads?
- I've never run an ad, can I really do this myself?
- How much time does this take each week? (answer teased: ~20 min)
- Will this work in my market / at my price point?
- Does this get sellers too, or just buyers?

### Q2, Second-Layer Questions (the "I didn't think of that" moments)
- Once leads come in, how do I not waste them / what do I say?
- How do I know if a campaign is working, or when to kill it?
- How is an AI-built ad actually different from boosting a post?
- Do these ads still work in a slow / shifting market?
- What's the real reason most agents' ads "fail"? (speed-to-lead)

### Q3, Objections (reasons not to show up / not to buy)
- "I don't have time to learn ad manager."
- "I threw money at Facebook before and got tire-kickers."
- "I already pay an agency / I tried an agency." *(handle WITHOUT knocking the agency)*
- "I'm not creative / not techy."
- "I'll just catch the replay." *(the live-attendance objection)*
- "$97 feels too cheap, what's the catch?"

### Q4, Expectations (frame the experience)
- What we'll cover: the 3 things that have to be true + a live AI ad build.
- It's LIVE: Q&A + a live-only bonus the replay won't have.
- Nothing to bring, just show up ready to watch us build one live.
- What's possible after: you can have your first campaign live within the week.

### Social proof bank (real, from salesgenius.co)
**Honesty rule (important):** these results came from the SalesGenius system run *by our agency for these clients*. Never imply the client ran their own ads or skipped the agency. Frame every proof point as: "our system did this, and AI is what now lets you run that same system yourself." This is more honest AND more on-strategy (it proves the system works and elevates the agency).
- Lisa Hall, $775K GCI in 2 yrs (and a $2.9M open-house buyer from a campaign we ran).
- Eric Palin, 7x return on ad spend in 2026 (we ran his campaigns; he did the follow-up).
- Rick Roccasalva, 146 new CRM contacts + showings from a campaign we launched for him.
- Skeptic-to-believer angle (use for the "tried it before" objection email).
- Phone Pro training praise ("great value, wow").
