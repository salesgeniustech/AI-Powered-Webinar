# THE ADS (final, ready to film): 9 scripts

*Selfie-style, 15 to 60 seconds each. Distinct angles on the Hormozi hooks, written direct-response clear (Kennedy / Bencivenga / Belcher): specific callout, problem in plain words, strong hook, compelling body, one clear CTA. Not clever. Clear. No em-dashes.*

**How to film:** phone at eye level or arm's length, look down the lens, natural light, talk like you're talking to one agent. First words are the hook, no slow intro. Vertical 9:16, captions on. One take per ad.

**The CTA (same shape every time, keeps the offer swappable):** drive the click, don't over-explain. Land on: *"Click this ad and save your seat. All the details are on the next page. It's free."* Add *"and we're doing it live"* where it fits. Because the details live on the registration page, you can change the offer any time without re-filming.

**The "why is an agency teaching this?" rule:** we ARE a real estate marketing agency, so we say it out loud and make it trust, not a contradiction (Ad 2 is built entirely on this). Never trash agencies, it undercuts our own high-ticket.

**Proof note:** the old Eric numbers were removed (no verified result to cite yet). When you have a real, permission-cleared client result, we add one proof ad, framed honestly (our system did it, AI now lets you run it yourself).

**Terminology:** these say "masterclass." The funnel pages still say "workshop." Say the word and I'll match them.
