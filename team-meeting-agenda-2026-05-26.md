## 3. The Two Things We Need to Improve On

The team crushed the pre-webinar setup last time. So this isn't a critique — it's about pushing two specific areas that will *materially* change the economics this round.

### A. Pre-Webinar Workflow → Increase Show Rates
Every percentage point of show rate is free revenue. Where we want to push:

- Tighter confirmation page after opt-in (set expectations, build anticipation)
- SMS reminder layer (24hr, 1hr, "we're live")
- Email reminder sequence with a hook in every send — not just "don't forget"
- Pre-frame video or one-sheet sent the night before so they show up warm
- A clear plan for no-shows → replay traffic → still get them to the offer

### B. Post-Sales-Call → Pre-Client Workflow
The window between "they paid $97 and booked a call with Chris" and "they say yes to $2k/month" is currently underbuilt. This is where we lose money. Where we want to push:

- Welcome email immediately on purchase (Skool invite, book-call CTA, what to expect)
- Case study email — build belief before the call
- Urgency / pre-call value email — keep them warm, reduce no-shows on Chris's calendar
- Pre-call intake form on Calendly so Chris walks in with context
- Confirmation page upgrade — Emma VSL + 1–2 case study videos + book-call CTA all in one place

If we win on show rate and we win on the post-purchase pre-call window, the same ad spend produces materially more closed retainers.
