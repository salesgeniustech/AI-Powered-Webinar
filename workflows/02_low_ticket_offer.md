## 1. The Offer in One Breath

> *"For $147, you get the exact paid-ad playbook we use to generate $40k in pipeline GCI for real estate clients — ad templates, landing page templates, follow-up sequences, AI prompts, plus 30 days inside our Skool community, plus a 30-minute implementation review call with our team after you've had time to run it. You walk out of this workshop with everything you need to start lead-genning on Monday."*

That's the line. Everyone on the team should be able to say it without reading it.

### What's actually in the box

| Deliverable | Format | Why it's in there |
|---|---|---|
| 3–5 ad creative templates | Copy + targeting specs + budget allocation | Removes the "what do I run?" blocker |
| 2 landing page templates | Full copy + form questions + CTA variations | Removes the "where do I send traffic?" blocker |
| 5–7 email + 3–5 SMS templates | Full sequence with timing | Removes the "what do I send after they opt in?" blocker |
| 10–15 AI prompts | Copy-paste, labelled by use case | This is the "AI-powered" part of the promise |
| CRM setup guide | Step-by-step | Removes the "where do I put the leads?" blocker |
| 30 days inside Skool | Community access | Implementation support, Q&A, our presence |
| 30-minute review call | Booked after Day 30 | The bridge into the $2k/mo retainer (Chris closes) |

### What is explicitly NOT in the box

So we don't over-promise on the call or in the emails:
- We don't run their ads for them. (That's the $2k/mo offer.)
- We don't build their landing pages for them. (Templates only.)
- We don't write their follow-up emails for them. (Templates only.)
- We don't guarantee $40k in 60 days at the $147 tier. (The $40k guarantee lives on the $2k/mo retainer only.)

This last one is critical. If anyone on the team — Chris, ops, support — accidentally extends the $40k/60-day guarantee to the $147 buyer, we will have refund requests we can't defend. The guarantee is the *door*, not the *deliverable* at this tier.

## 2. When the Offer Gets Presented (Webinar Timing)

The full webinar is 60 minutes. The offer gets presented in the final 5-minute close block, but the *seeds* are planted throughout. Here's the timing map:

| Minute | What's happening | What the offer is doing |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | Opening — problem + promise | (Seed) — the promise *is* the offer's job-to-be-done |
| 5–20 | Pillar 1: Ad Setup | (Seed) — "the exact ad templates I'm showing you are in the playbook" |
| 20–35 | Pillar 2: Landing Page | (Seed) — "the page template I'm showing you is in the playbook" |
| 35–50 | Pillar 3: Follow-Up | (Seed) — "the full sequence is in the playbook" |
| 50–55 | Pillar 4: Math + Proof | (Tee-up) — "here's what's possible if you run this system" |
| 55–57 | Social proof | (Tee-up) — "these are the buyers who ran the playbook" |
| **57–60** | **The close — LTO** | **The actual pitch** |
| (after) | High-ticket tee-up | "Most playbook buyers end up on a call with us within 30 days…" |

**The teaching is the demo.** Every pillar names a deliverable that's inside the playbook. By the time we hit minute 57, the buyer has already seen the templates — they're paying for the version they don't have to rebuild.

### The 3-minute close script (skeleton — Emma to humanize)

1. **Recap** (30 sec): *"You just saw the ad structure, the landing page, the follow-up sequence, and the math. The next thing you need is the assets."*
2. **Reveal** (30 sec): *"That's what the Playbook is. Everything I just walked you through, ready to copy."*
3. **Stack** (60 sec): walk the deliverables list, name the dollar value of each, total it up.
4. **Price + urgency** (30 sec): *"$147 today, only for people on this workshop. Goes to $297 after."*
5. **Risk reversal** (15 sec): *"If you open it and it's not exactly what I described, email us and we'll refund you. No friction."*
6. **CTA** (15 sec): *"Link's in the chat. Grab it now while we open Q&A."*

## 3. Common Objections + How To Handle Them

These come up in the chat during the close, in DMs after, and in emails to the admin inbox. The team should be able to answer all of them in their own voice — these aren't scripts, they're the underlying logic.

### Objection 1 — *"I can find this stuff for free on YouTube."*
**What they're really saying:** "I don't yet believe the assembly is worth $147 vs. the parts being free."
**The handle:** Agree, then reframe. *"You can. And you'd spend 40 hours stitching it together and still not know if the targeting works in a soft market. The playbook is the assembled version — tested, in order, with the prompts that go with it. The $147 is the time you don't spend assembling."*

### Objection 2 — *"Will this work in [my market / Canada / a small town / a soft market]?"*
**What they're really saying:** "I want permission to believe this is for me."
**The handle:** *"The system is built for $600k+ average markets. If your average is under $400k, the unit economics don't work and we'll tell you that up front. If you're in a $600k+ market, it works — we've run it in soft markets, hot markets, and Canadian markets. The Skool community has examples from each."*
**Don't:** Promise it works everywhere. Disqualify the wrong-market buyer at $147 instead of refunding them at day 14.

### Objection 3 — *"Is the $40k in 60 days guaranteed at this price?"*
**What they're really saying:** "I'm anchoring on the headline number and want it for $147."
**The handle (critical to get right):** *"The $40k/60-day guarantee is on the done-with-you retainer — that's where we run the ads ourselves. The Playbook gives you the system; the guarantee covers the execution. Most playbook buyers come back to us within 30 days for the retainer once they realize they'd rather we ran it for them."*
**Don't:** Imply the guarantee applies at $147. It doesn't. (See section 1 — this is the biggest legal/refund risk on the offer.)

### Objection 4 — *"Can I just book the $2k/month call without buying the playbook?"*
**What they're really saying:** "I'm ready to skip ahead."
**The handle:** *"The review call is part of the playbook because we want you to have run something before we talk — it makes the call 10x more useful. If you're already running ads and want to skip ahead, reply to this email and we'll route you straight to Chris."*
**Why this matters:** A hot, $2k-ready buyer should never be slowed down by our funnel. Let them skip. Just don't advertise that path.

### Objection 5 — *"How much time do I need to implement this?"*
**The handle:** *"Plan for one focused weekend to set it up — ads, page, sequence — and then 30 minutes a day to optimize. The AI prompts are the part that compresses what used to take a week into an afternoon."*

### Objection 6 — *"I bought the playbook — when does my call get booked?"*
(This is the bottleneck from last round — the *operational* one, not a sales objection.)
**The handle:** *"The review call is intentionally booked 30 days after purchase so you've had time to run the system. You'll get the Calendly link on Day 28. In the meantime, Skool is open today — that's where the support happens."*
**Why this matters:** Last round, buyers expected an *immediate* call and the admin inbox flooded with "where's my call?" emails. This needs to be in Email 1, on the confirmation page, and in the Skool welcome post. Repeated three times.

### Objection 7 — *"Refund?"*
**The handle:** *"Yes — if you open the playbook and it's not what I described in the workshop, reply to the receipt email and we'll refund you. No friction."*
**Internal rule:** Refund window is 14 days. Any refund request inside 14 days is processed without argument. After 14 days, refunds are at the team's discretion and tied to non-delivery only.

### Objection 8 — *"Do I need to be technical / good at AI to use this?"*
**The handle:** *"No. The AI prompts are copy-paste — you paste them into ChatGPT or Claude, change two things, and you're done. If you can edit a Google Doc, you can run the playbook."*

## 4. Delivery & Fulfillment — The Anti-Bottleneck Plan

This is the section that last round didn't have. The team felt blindsided because the *sale* happened before *fulfillment* was nailed down. Fix:

### The moment of purchase
The instant `checkout.session.completed` fires on Stripe, three things must happen — automated, no human:

1. **Receipt email** sent by Stripe.
2. **Welcome / access email** sent by the ESP, containing:
   - Skool invite link (single-use)
   - Direct download link to the playbook (or a dashboard URL)
   - "Your 30-minute review call gets booked on Day 28 — we'll email you" (set the expectation)
3. **CRM tag** applied: `lto-buyer` + purchase date.

If any of those three fail, the admin inbox lights up. The smoke test on Day 8 must verify all three.

### Day 1–28 (the silent middle)
The buyer should never feel forgotten in this window. Cadence:

- **Day 1:** Welcome email (above)
- **Day 2:** Case study email (build belief — pulls from `content/case_studies/`)
- **Day 5:** "How are you doing?" — point them to Skool
- **Day 14:** Mid-point check-in (Skool post + email)
- **Day 28:** Calendly link drops for the review call

If we go silent for 14+ days, refunds spike. (This was the pattern last round.)

### Day 28+ — the review call
- Calendly link emails to the buyer with a pre-call intake form.
- Intake form asks: what they ran, what worked, what didn't, what's blocking them. Chris walks in with context.
- Call is 30 minutes. First 20: review their results. Last 10: pitch $2k/mo if they're a fit.
- Setter SOP for Chris lives in `workflows/09_setter_sop_for_chris.md` (separate doc).

### Who owns what when something breaks

| Failure mode | Owner | Response time |
|---|---|---|
| Stripe webhook didn't fire | Ops / Claude Code | Within 1 hour (check `stripe-webhook.js` logs in Netlify) |
| Buyer didn't get welcome email | Ops | Within 4 hours — manual resend from ESP |
| Skool invite link broken | Emma | Within 24 hours — regenerate invite |
| Refund request | Ops | Same day — process if inside 14 days |
| "Where's my call?" email | Ops | Same day — reply with Day 28 expectation copy |
| Buyer is hot for $2k/mo before Day 28 | Ops escalates to Chris | Within 24 hours |
