# Welcome Video Script — welcome.html (inhouseteam.salesgenius.co)
**Placement:** Hero video on the post-purchase Welcome page ("Watch First: How Your 30-Day Install Works")
**Length:** ~90 seconds. Face to camera, one take, warm and quick — they already bought. Confirm, direct, get out.
**Jobs:** 1) confirm the decision, 2) get the 3 steps done today, 3) set the show-up expectation.
**Language source of truth:** AI_Team_Foundational_Positioning_Doc.pdf

---

## THE SCRIPT

**(0:00–0:20) Welcome + confirmation**

Hey — Emma here. Welcome to the Founder's Cohort. You're one of just <10> agents in this first round, and here's the short version of what you just did: you hired three employees. An AI Executive Assistant, an AI Sales Manager inside your Follow Up Boss or Lofty, and an AI Ads Manager — $150k a year of staff if they were human. In 30 days, all three are installed and working. We do it with you, live, one install per week.

**(0:20–1:05) The three steps — do them now**

Right below this video are three steps. Fifteen minutes total — do them now, while you're here.

One: the intake form. That's how we customize your install around YOUR database and deal flow before Session 1.

Two: put all four sessions in your calendar right now, before your schedule eats them. Remember the guarantee — show up to three of the four, do the simple homework, and your install is guaranteed. Those four time slots ARE the guarantee. Protect them like listing appointments.

Three: check your email. Our team is sending you everything you need — your access to the community, your tools, all of it. If it's not there in the next few minutes, check spam and drag us to your inbox — you don't want to miss what's coming over the next 30 days. And when you get into the community, introduce yourself: your market, and your 30-day goal.

**(1:05–1:30) Send-off**

That's it. Do the three steps, then take the rest of the day off from this — we've got it from here. I'll see you at Session 1, where you hire your first employee. You're going to love Monday mornings by the end of this.

— Emma

## BEFORE YOU RECORD

1. Insert the real seat count; if dates are locked, say the Session 1 date out loud.
2. Point down when you say "right below this video" — it lifts step completion.
3. One take energy. Warmth beats polish on post-purchase videos.
4. NO upsell mention — Week 3 is the first seed, per Doc 2.
