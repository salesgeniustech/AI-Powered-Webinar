## Workshop Overview

- **Title:** How to Run AI-Powered Paid Ad Campaigns for Real Estate Lead Gen (In a Soft Market)
- **Format:** 60-minute live webinar/workshop
- **Delivery:** Live on Zoom (recorded for VSL/replay)
- **Target audience:** Realtors making $150k+ in $600k+ average price markets who want to scale to $500k using paid ads
- **Core promise:** Generate $40,000 in pipeline GCI in 60 days without burning your ad budget in a soft market

## Who This Is For

### Primary Avatar
- Realtors making $150k–$300k annually
- Operating in markets with $600k+ average home prices
- Struggling in a soft market (fewer leads, longer sales cycles)
- Willing to spend money on ads but scared of wasting it
- Open to AI solutions but skeptical about hype
- Want predictable, scalable lead generation
- Don't have a sales team (solo or small team)

### Secondary Avatar
- Real estate teams/brokers looking to scale lead gen
- Realtors who've tried ads before but didn't get results
- Realtors who are tech-forward and want to automate

### Who This Is NOT For
- Realtors in sub-$400k markets (the economics don't work)
- Realtors not willing to implement
- Realtors looking for quick fixes without work

## Workshop Structure (60 Minutes)

### Opening (5 min)
**Objective:** Hook them on the problem and the promise.

- Acknowledge the soft market pain (fewer leads, longer cycles, scared to spend)
- Position the opportunity (AI + paid ads = predictable lead flow even in soft market)
- State the promise: *"In the next hour, I'm going to show you exactly how to generate $40k in pipeline GCI in 60 days without burning your ad budget"*
- Quick credibility: *"We've done this with X clients, here are the results..."*

**Tone:** Direct, empathetic, confident.

---

### Pillar 1 — The AI-Powered Ad Setup (15 min)
**Objective:** Show them the exact ad structure and how AI makes it faster/better.

- The problem most realtors have with ads (wrong targeting, bad creative, no optimization)
- Your ad structure (what works in soft market)
    - Budget allocation (how much to spend per ad set)
    - Targeting strategy (who to target in $600k+ markets)
    - Creative strategy (what types of ads convert)
- How AI speeds this up
    - Use AI to write ad copy variations (show exact prompts)
    - Use AI to test headlines and messaging
    - Use AI to optimize targeting based on performance
- Real example: show a client's ad account
    - Before: generic ads, low CTR
    - After: AI-optimized ads, higher CTR
    - Results: cost per lead, conversion rate

**LTO deliverable:** Ad templates + AI prompts they can use immediately.
**Tone:** Practical, show-don't-tell, specific examples.

---

### Pillar 2 — The Landing Page & Lead Capture (15 min)
**Objective:** Show them how to capture qualified leads on the front end.

- The problem most realtors have (generic landing pages, low conversion, unqualified leads)
- Your landing page structure
    - Headline (what works in soft market)
    - Benefits (what realtors actually care about)
    - Form questions (how to qualify on the front end)
    - CTA (what converts)
- How AI helps with landing page optimization
    - Test different headlines
    - Personalize messaging by market
    - Score leads based on form responses
- Real example: client's landing page
    - Before: generic, low conversion
    - After: optimized, higher conversion
    - Results: conversion rate, lead quality

**LTO deliverable:** Landing page template + form questions + copy variations.
**Tone:** Practical, show the actual pages, explain the "why" behind each element.

---

### Pillar 3 — The Follow-Up System (15 min)
**Objective:** Show them how to nurture and convert leads to calls.

- The problem most realtors have (leads go cold, no follow-up, low conversion)
- Your follow-up system
    - Email sequence (what to send, when to send it)
    - SMS strategy (when to use SMS vs. email)
    - CRM setup (how to organize leads)
    - Timing (when to follow up for best results)
- How AI helps with follow-up
    - Personalize emails at scale
    - Score leads (who to follow up with first)
    - Write follow-up copy
- Real example: client's follow-up sequence
    - Email 1, 2, 3 (actual copy)
    - Results (open rate, click rate, conversion to call)
    - Show the CRM setup

**LTO deliverable:** Email templates + SMS templates + CRM setup guide + AI prompts for personalization.
**Tone:** Practical, show actual sequences, explain the psychology behind each email.

---

### Pillar 4 — The Math & Proof (10 min)
**Objective:** Prove that $40k in 60 days is real and achievable.

- The math that works in soft market
    - Cost per lead (target: $10–15)
    - Lead to call conversion (target: 20–30%)
    - Call to client conversion (target: 30–50%)
    - Average deal size ($40k pipeline GCI = 2–3 clients at $700k homes)
- Real client case studies (2–3)
    - Client A: Started at $0, hit $40k in 60 days
    - Client B: Was doing ads wrong, switched to this system, hit $60k in 60 days
    - Client C: In soft market, still hit $40k in 60 days
- Show the actual numbers: ad spend, leads, calls booked, clients closed, pipeline GCI

**Tone:** Data-driven, credible, specific numbers.

---

### Social Proof (5 min)
**Objective:** Build trust and urgency.

- 2–3 short testimonials (video clips or quotes)
- Before/after results
- What they say about working with you
- Quick mention of the guarantee: $40k in 60 days or you don't pay

**Tone:** Authentic, specific results.

2. **AI as efficiency tool:** *"AI isn't magic. It's a tool that makes you faster and smarter. We show you exactly how to use it."*
3. **Proven system:** *"This isn't theory. We've done this with X clients. Here are the results."*
4. **Guarantee:** *"$40k in 60 days or you don't pay. We're that confident it works."*
5. **Implementation focus:** *"We don't just teach theory. We give you templates, prompts, and systems you can use today."*

---

## What NOT To Do

- Don't oversell AI. It's a tool, not magic.
- Don't make it too complicated. Keep it simple and actionable.
- Don't spend too much time on theory. Show real examples.
- Don't forget to qualify. Make it clear this is for $600k+ markets.
- Don't pitch too hard. Let the results speak.
- Don't make promises you can't keep. Stick to what you've proven.
