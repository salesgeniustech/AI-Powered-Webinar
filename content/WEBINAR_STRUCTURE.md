# WEBINAR STRUCTURE, Drawers & Folders

*Filing-cabinet outline (drawers → folders; files/slides come later). Reverse-engineered from `content/THE_OFFER.md`. Goal of this doc: give Emma enough to build the funnel, registration page, thank-you page, VSLs.*

*Built on the traditional webinar structure (Intro → 3 belief drawers → Stack → Close). The fix vs. the old deck: collapse 5 taught skills into 3 belief drawers so we stop overteaching and start belief-shifting.*

---

## The reverse-engineer (this is the whole logic)

Every drawer breaks ONE belief, teaches just enough to make it feel possible, demos the tool as the shortcut, and pre-sells a specific offer component.

| Drawer | Belief it breaks | Tool reveal | Offer piece it pre-sells |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1, The vehicle | "Running my own Meta ads is too complex/expensive, that's agency work." | AI builds the targeting in one click | **Core: AI Ad Machine** |
| 2, The creative | "I'm not techy or creative enough to make ads that work." | AI writes the offer, copy + creative live | **Core tool** (+ lightly, the **AI Marketing Toolkit**) |
| 3, The time & the trust | "I don't have time, the leads will be junk, and I won't know if it's working." | Autopilot + lead alerts + the scorecard | **Ad Scorecard System**, **Phone Conversion Kit**, **Launch Crew**, and teases the **Voice Caller OTO** |

## What changed from your existing deck

- **5 skills → 3 belief drawers.** You taught Find Your Pond / Cast Your Bait / Write The Words / Stop The Scroll / Launch Your Ads as five full lessons. They're now **folders** regrouped under 3 drawers, each anchored to a belief.
- **Kept your strongest cold-open hooks** verbatim, they're doing real work.
- **Kept all 5 skill names**, demoted from drawers to folders, so the branding survives.
- **Rebuilt the close.** The old ending (AdGenius + Prompt Pack + One Month Free) doesn't reflect the locked stack. The new close walks the real offer.
- **Teach less, demo more.** Inside each folder: one line of concept to break the belief, then the tool does the work. The deep how-to lives in the paid product/Bootcamp bonus, not on stage.

## OPENING, Hook & Big Domino *(before the drawers)*

Folders:
- **Cold open**, your existing run: The Barrier Dropped → 1 Lead, Four Agents → 400 Clicks, Zero Leads → $45+ Per Lead, Not Yours → Your Own Leads, On Your Terms.
- **Name the 3 pains in their words**, "I threw money at Facebook and got nothing" / "all I got were tire-kickers" / "I don't have time to learn ad manager."
- **The Big Domino**, *you can get your own quality leads on demand, without an agency, without being techy, for less than one retainer, because AI now builds the ads for you.*
- **Quick credibility**, the agency, the brokerages you're paid to teach.
- **The one case study**, a realtor (your own ad account counts): zero ads → AI-built ads → real leads, with numbers. *Make-or-break; don't skip.*
- **The frame**, "I'll teach you to do this yourself. Or you can let our tool do it. Both work."
- **Agenda**, "3 things that have to be true for this to work for you."

## DRAWER 1, "The vehicle isn't broken, you just never had the keys"

**Belief to break:** running your own Meta ads is too complicated/expensive; it's agency stuff.

Folders:
- **Find Your Pond**, it's not about more money, it's about fishing where the buyers/sellers are.
- **Teach (just enough):** who your buyer is + the right targeting. One concept, not a course.
- **Tool reveal:** the AI builds your buyer profile + targeting in one click.
- **Belief landed:** "the hard part was the setup, and the AI just did it."

*Pre-sells: the core AI Ad Machine.*

---

## DRAWER 2, "You don't need to be techy or creative, the AI is"

**Belief to break:** I can't write ads, design creative, or build an offer, I'm not creative/technical.

Folders:
- **Cast Your Bait**, the offer/lead magnet that actually pulls leads (concept only).
- **Write The Words**, ad copy has 3 jobs: hook, body, CTA (one line each).
- **Stop The Scroll**, what makes a creative stop the thumb (one concept).
- **Tool reveal:** AI generates the bait, the copy, and the creative, live, in front of them.
- **Belief landed:** "the part I was most scared of is the part the AI does best."

*Pre-sells: the core tool (does all three); lightly teases the AI Marketing Toolkit for what happens after the lead comes in.*

---

## DRAWER 3, "You don't need more time, and you'll always know it's working"

**Belief to break:** I don't have time, the leads will be junk, and I won't know if the campaign is working or how to fix it.

Folders:
- **Launch Your Ads**, the 20-min/week routine; the 2 rules for your first launch; the before-you-publish check (compress your old checklist).
- **Know Your Numbers**, how to tell at a glance if a campaign is winning, and what to change if it's not. *(This is the belief the Ad Scorecard System removes.)*
- **Speed-to-Lead**, a fresh lead is hot for seconds; what to say when they pick up + the follow-up. *(Pre-sells the Phone Conversion Kit + Toolkit; sets up the Voice Caller OTO.)*
- **Tool reveal:** autopilot + instant lead alerts + the scorecard view.
- **Belief landed:** "I can run this in 20 minutes a week and never wonder if it's working."

*Pre-sells: Ad Scorecard System, Phone Conversion Kit, Launch Crew; teases Voice Caller.*

## THE CLOSE, Stack & Sell

Folders:
- **Restate the Big Domino**, "if you believe those 3 things, this is the only thing you need."
- **The shortcut reveal**, "now let me show you AdGenius" (your existing transition works).
- **Walk the stack**, tie each piece back to the drawer/belief it solves: core tool (Drawers 1-2), Toolkit (lead follow-up), Ad Scorecard System (Drawer 3 confidence), Phone Conversion Kit (the call), Founders Launch Crew (you'll actually launch).
- **Fast-action bonus**, Phone Pro Masterclass, *only if you join before we close out today.*
- **Guarantee**, ads live in 7 days; no first lead in 14 → next month free.
- **Scarcity**, 20 spots, because the Launch Crew groups stay small.
- **Price reveal**, $97/mo, locked for life.
- **Handle the 3 objections**, too good to be true / will it work for *me* / is now the time.
- **CTA → checkout.**
- **OTO tease**, "you'll see one optional upgrade on the next page, grab your $97 spot first." (Voice Caller.)
- **Q&A that re-sells**, seed questions that re-walk the stack and scarcity.

## Funnel asset implications (so you can build now)

- **Registration page**, headline = the Big Domino. Use the cold-open hooks as section headers ("400 Clicks. Zero Leads." etc.). Promise = what they'll believe by the end, not what they'll be taught.
- **Thank-you / confirmation page**, the case study + a tease of the 3 drawers + the *live-only fast-action bonus* to drive show-rate (this is your #1 profit lever).
- **VSL (registration or replay)**, compress to: cold open → Big Domino → Drawer 1 belief → case study → the frame → CTA to attend. Don't teach; just make them believe attending is worth it.
- **Reminder sequence**, each email/SMS = one drawer's belief as a curiosity hook, plus the live-only bonus reminder.
