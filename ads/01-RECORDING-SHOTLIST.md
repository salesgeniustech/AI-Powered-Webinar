## A1, Short-form hooks (15 to 40 sec), your volume drivers

⭐ **C1, The 2-minute build** *(also works as a demo, see Setup B)*
> Hook: "I just built a Facebook ad for my real estate business in under two minutes."
> I didn't hire a designer. I didn't call an agency. The AI wrote the offer, wrote the copy, and made the image. I just approved it. I'm a realtor, not a tech person. I'm running a free live workshop where I show you how to do this yourself. [CTA]

⭐ **C2, Stop boosting**
> Hook: "Realtors, stop hitting the boost button."
> That button is built to get you cheap likes, not buyers. That's why you end up with 200 names in your CRM and zero booked appointments. Real campaigns target the people about to buy or sell, and AI now builds those for you. I'll show you how, live and free. [CTA]

⭐ **C3, Tire-kickers**
> Hook: "If you're tired of paying for real estate leads that never call you back, watch this."
> The problem was never you. It was the build: wrong targeting, weak offer, boring creative. AI fixes all three now. Come to my free workshop and I'll show you how to get your own leads, the kind that actually answer. [CTA]

⭐ **C4, No agency needed**
> Hook: "You used to need a two thousand dollar a month agency to run real estate ads. Not anymore."
> AI builds the ad. You run it in about twenty minutes a week. I'll teach you the whole thing on a free live workshop. Your leads, your account, your terms. [CTA]

⭐ **C5, It wasn't you**

> They flopped because of three things every agent used to get wrong, and AI now does all three for you. I'm building one live on a free workshop so you can see the difference. [CTA]

⭐ **C6, Speed to lead**
