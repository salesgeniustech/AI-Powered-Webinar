## SHOOTING TIPS

### Setup (Same for All Pieces)
- **Location:** Clean background, good lighting
- **Camera:** Phone camera is fine
- **Audio:** Quiet room, good microphone
- **Framing:** Head and shoulders, eye contact with camera
- **Outfit:** Professional but not stuffy

### Batch Shooting Strategy
- **Monday:** Shoot all Authority pieces (1-8) in one location
- **Tuesday:** Shoot all Social Proof pieces (9-16) - may need client testimonials
- **Wednesday:** Shoot all Objection Handling pieces (17-24) in one location
- **Thursday:** Shoot all Product Awareness pieces (25-32) in one location
- **Friday:** Shoot all Belief Management pieces (33-40) in one location

### Editing Tips
- **Captions:** Add captions for every piece (people watch without sound)
- **Graphics:** Add title card at beginning, CTA at end
- **Music:** Use royalty-free background music (low volume)
- **Length:** Keep to specified time (2-5 min)
- **Format:** Export as vertical video (9:16) for social media
