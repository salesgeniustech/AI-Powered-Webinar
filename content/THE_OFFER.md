## Guarantee

**Conditional / Hormozi-style, risk on the customer to act.** *You* publish your ads within 7 days (we help on the Launch Crew call). If you get them live and still don't have a lead within 14 days, you get your next month free **and** a private ad-review call to fix what's off.

The customer must publish to qualify, that's what puts the risk on them: it forces activation and protects against buyers who do nothing. The Launch Crew + guarantee are the same mechanism doing double duty: forced activation = churn defense on an MRR play, plus a qualified (not blanket) guarantee.
