# AD HOOK LIBRARY (Hormozi buckets, adapted to our offer)

*A test bank of scroll-stopping hook openers, organized by Alex Hormozi's 7 proven hook types. The SOP wants many hooks tested, this is that bank. Film the hook opener, add one line of mechanism or proof, then land the clear promise and CTA. No em-dashes. Honest proof rule applies (our system did it; AI lets you run it yourself).*

---

## THE CLEAR PROMISE (say it plainly in every ad)
**If you want to generate your own buyer and seller leads without hiring an agency, you can now just use AI to run your own ads. On a free live workshop, I'll show you exactly how.**

Short tag: *Run your own ads. Get your own leads. No agency. AI does the hard part.*

## THE STRUCTURE every ad follows
1. **Hook opener** (pick one below, first 3 seconds).
2. **One line of mechanism or proof** (AI builds the ad / our system took an agent from X to Y).
3. **The clear promise** (the line above).
4. **CTA:** "Save your free seat, link below." or "Comment ADS and I'll send you the link."

Keep the promise identical across every ad. The hook is the only thing that changes. That is what lets you test cleanly and find winners.

---

## 1. Curiosity & Controversy
- **Rumors:** "The rumors are true. Realtors are running their own Facebook ads now, without an agency, and AI is doing the hard part."
- **What If I Told You:** "What if I told you the part of running ads you hate, the targeting, the copy, the image, is now done by AI in about two minutes?"
- **Is It Possible:** "Is it possible to get your own buyer and seller leads without an agency and without being techy? It is, and I'll prove it."
- **Quick Question:** "Quick question. How much did you spend on real estate leads last month that never picked up the phone?"
- **In the Next 60 Seconds:** "In the next sixty seconds I'll show you how realtors are running their own AI ads in about twenty minutes a week."

## 2. Comparison & Contrast
- **Comparison:** "Agency-run ads versus your own AI ads. Same leads. One costs two thousand a month. The other costs you twenty minutes a week."
- **Smart vs Dumb:** "Smart agents own their leads. Everyone else rents them from Zillow and splits them with the brokerage."
- **$75K vs $220K agent:** "The seventy-five thousand dollar agent says 'I need more leads.' The two hundred twenty thousand dollar agent says 'I have my own lead system.'"
- **Broke vs Rich:** "Struggling agents wait for leads. Top producers turn theirs on like a tap."
- **X vs X, what's the difference:** "Boosting a post versus running a real campaign. Same money. Completely different people show up."

## 3. Story & Personal Experience
- **If I Woke Up:** "If I lost my entire pipeline tomorrow and had to start from scratch, here's the very first thing I'd do to get leads."
- **If I Only Had:** "If I only had twenty dollars a day and twenty minutes a week to get my own real estate leads, this is exactly what I'd do."
- **It Took Me X Years:** "It took me a decade in real estate to learn that the agents who win are the ones who own their leads. AI just made that easy."
- **I Went From This to This:** "We took an agent from seventy-five thousand to two hundred twenty thousand GCI by getting him his own leads. Here's the system."
- **My Client Got Results:** "One agent got a hundred and forty-six new people in their CRM from a single campaign we ran. That's the same system I'm about to teach you."

## 4. Data, Math & Proof
- **Economics / Data:** "Agent A pays an agency two thousand a month. Agent B runs the same ads with AI for just the ad spend. Guess who keeps more of every commission."
- **Pattern Recognition:** "Every realtor who generates their own leads does the same three things. AI now does two of them for you."
- **Did You Know:** "Did you know an online lead is hot for about five minutes? Most agents call back the next day and wonder why nobody answers."
- **Prediction / Future:** "Here's what happens in the next year. The agents running their own AI ads eat everyone else's lunch. Let me show you how to be one of them."

## 5. Authority & Teaching
- **Everyone Tells You:** "Everyone tells realtors to 'run Facebook ads.' Nobody actually shows you how. I will, for free."
- **More Than a Degree:** "This free workshop will teach you more about getting your own leads than the coaching I've been paid five thousand dollars to deliver at brokerages."
- **This Is Your Business:** "This is your pipeline when you wait for leads. And this is your pipeline when you run your own. Let me show you the second one."
- **Only X You Need:** "There are only three things you need to get your own real estate leads. AI handles two of them. I'll teach you all three."

## 6. Problem, Mistake & Solution
- **Mistake / Problem:** "Most realtors make the same mistake on Facebook. They boost a post and call it advertising."
- **What Happens When:** "What happens when you stop renting leads and start owning them? Your business stops depending on anyone else."
- **Does Your Business Look Like This:** "Does your lead flow look like this? Dead some months, slammed others. Here's how to make it steady."
- **If You Want Results:** "If you want your own steady stream of buyer and seller leads, you really only need to fix one thing: who is building your ads."
- **Competitive Advantage:** "Agents who own their leads versus agents who wait for referrals. Here's the difference, and how to switch sides."

## 7. Lifestyle & Emotional
- **Top Producer / Status:** "Want to be the top producer in your office? It starts with owning your own lead flow."
- **If You're X:** "If you're a realtor already doing six figures but your pipeline still keeps you up at night, this is for you."
- **Lifestyle / Emotional:** "Imagine never starting a month at zero. Never wondering where the next deal comes from. That's what owning your leads feels like."
- **Case Study / Proof:** "I watched an agent go from living off scraps to seven hundred seventy-five thousand GCI in two years, once she owned her own leads."

## How to use this with the shot list
The talking-head hooks in `01-RECORDING-SHOTLIST.md` (the C-series) are still good; these expand the bank so you can test widely. Film a batch of these openers in one sitting, all landing on the same clear promise and CTA. The hooks that survive the first 48 hours are your winners. Kill the rest, and never try to revive a dead hook by changing the audience. Change the hook, not the audience.
